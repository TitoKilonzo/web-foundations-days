About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple place to jot down the small things you don't want to forget, like a to-do, a half-formed idea or a reminder. It stays out of your way, so you can type a thought, save it and get back to what you were doing.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Click the "New note" box and type what you want to remember.
  3. Press Enter or click the "Add Note" button.
  4. Find your note in the "Your notes" list below the form.

Features

  • Add a note in seconds
  • See all your notes in one tidy list
  • A running count of how many notes you have
  • Works with just a keyboard

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note you just typed
Tab Move to the next field or button
Shift + Tab Move back to the previous field or button
